# Pinnacle Roadmap

Welcome to the roadmap for Pinnacle! This document outlines our goals and the features we are working on to make Pinnacle the premier autonomous business intelligence platform.

## Q3 2026
- **Core AI Integration**: Connect the frontend dashboard with basic AI agents for market research queries.
- **Data Visualization Setup**: Integrate charting libraries to visualize initial data models.
- **User Authentication**: Implement secure login and user role management.

## Q4 2026
- **Advanced Agent Collaboration**: Enable multiple specialized agents (e.g., Researcher, Analyst, Strategist) to collaborate on a single complex business query.
- **Custom Report Generation**: Allow users to export generated strategies to PDF and interactive web formats.
- **Competitor Tracking Dashboard**: A dedicated view for continuous real-time tracking of designated competitors.

## Q1 2027
- **Predictive Modeling**: Introduce predictive insights based on historical market data.
- **API Access**: Release a public API for enterprise users to integrate Pinnacle's agents into their internal workflows.
- **Team Workspaces**: Enable collaborative workspaces for teams to share and discuss AI-generated strategy reports.

*Note: This roadmap is a living document and is subject to change based on user feedback and technological advancements.*
